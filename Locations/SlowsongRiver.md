# Slowsong River
Slowsong River is a massive water-filled valley stretching from the northernmost coast to the southernmost chain of the Elder Mountain Range. It is almost an entire kilometre wide and is quite a fall from the top to the water level.

Around the southern segment, there is a stone bridge built across the chasm, but it is without lighting. Despite being a beautiful spot, highwaymen tend to use this landmark to their own advantage, making it risky for anyone who crosses it.
