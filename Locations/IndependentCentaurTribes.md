# Independent Centaur Tribes
Although there is no specific landscape or any established buildings in this place, the warfare is interesting enough to be documented. In this land roam tens of different nomadic centaur tribes, all contesting against each other in skirmishes for control of this land.

It is extremely dangerous to cross this land, as they are likely to encounter a tribe that will not be very happy with foreigners standing on what they see as their land.

That being said, the forests in this region have tall, thick trees, while forest edges have shorter, lighter-looking trees. Despite there not being much undergrowth, these forests can get surprisingly dark.
