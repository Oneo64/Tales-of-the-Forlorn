# Contested Territories
Several nomadic centaur tribes roam the land between the deserts and the eastern meadows, all constantly fighting each other for power over this strip of land. For travellers, it is extremely dangerous to cross this land, as they can easily be mistaken for enemies.

That being said, the forests in this region have tall, thick trees, while forest edges have shorter, lighter-looking trees. Despite there not being much undergrowth, these forests can get surprisingly dark.
