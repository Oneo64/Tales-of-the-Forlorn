# Crafthall City
Crafthall City is a large settlement built along a long hilltop. The city itself is around a hundred meters wide and several hundred meters long, which tapers down until only one street (the main road) remains, giving it considerable space for trade and residence. Because of how the width changes, things gradually get more expensive the deeper you go into the city.

At the end of the city road, the road continues into the keep of Redfall, built on an almost separate hill not far from the city. Because of how the land is shaped, the only way to get into the keep is by this road.
