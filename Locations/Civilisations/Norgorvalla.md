The Kingdom of Norgorvalla is a major kingdom in the northern lands of the known world. Although powerful, the city of Hvatingvarde is the only major city in the kingdom; other settlements are semi-independent villages and townlets scattered across the lands.

## Architecture
Unlike other civilisations, Norgorvalla has preserved its traditional culture in architecture.

Village buildings are fully wooden longhouses with sod roofing. They often have just one floor, and have a small enclosure for farm animals and orchards. Lighting usually comes from a long central hearth in the middle of the longhouse, with smoke expelled from a small opening in the ceiling. Outside lighting is often lit by standing torches, and sometimes, lanterns.

City buildings are of course more refined, and are more like houses than halls. Although walls are often made of wood and are covered with clay, some buildings' first-floor walls are instead made of stone. Roofs are made using simple clay tiles. Lighting usually comes from lanterns (and sometimes a square hearth), but richer households/buildings may have braziers.
