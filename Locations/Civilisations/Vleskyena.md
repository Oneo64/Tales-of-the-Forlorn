The Kingdom of Vleskyena is a minor kingdom in the northern lands of the known world.

## Architecture
Although architecture in Vleskyenic Culture has remained traditional, it has taken a different turn from the norm.

Vleskyenic houses are built using sturdy wood. Unlike in other cultures, it is common for houses (especially for business) to have two floors. Roofs are usually built steeply using thick thatch or clay tiles.

At the capital fortress, Vleskyena, towers are built round and have conical tiled roofs, while the curtain wall also has a roof of its own.
