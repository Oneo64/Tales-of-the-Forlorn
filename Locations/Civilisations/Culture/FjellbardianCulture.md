# Fjellbardian culture
Fjellbardian culture, although similar to Norgorvallian culture, has been heavily influenced by Redmannish culture.

## Virtues
Bravery and honesty are some of the most important virtues in Fjellbardian culture.

## Architecture
Compared to its two neighbouring kingdoms, Fjellbardian culture has sacrificed might and power for the comfort of its civilians. Almost every settlement in its land is a town, whether big or small.

Fjellbardic houses are often made using pine logs with thick stone bases, due to the abundance of it near the mountain. They typically have sod roofs and larger windows with wooden shutters, sometimes with an inner locking mechanism for wealthier households.
