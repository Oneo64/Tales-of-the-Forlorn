# Mÿldassïan culture
Although originally descended from the elves of the south, Mÿldassïan culture has lost so much of its original virtue that it has become something entirely different.

## Virtues
Mÿldassïan culture emphasises elegance and etherealness, but the people can also be just as ruthless as their neighbour cultures.

Because of these values, some may consider themselves hardened by the true nature of the world, compared to their southern counterparts.

## Religion
Moon worship is the most widespread in Mÿldassïan culture. They associate the moon with the god Ainassare, and sometimes, monks will make a pilgrimage to sacred shrines dedicated to it.

## Architecture
Mÿldassïan buildings are often made of wood, with stone bases. Their roofs are usually built slightly steeper than others, using strips of cured wood with the bark still on.
