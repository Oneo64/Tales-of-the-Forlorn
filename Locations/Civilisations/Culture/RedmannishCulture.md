# Redmannish culture
Redmannish culture is very similar to the real-world West Germanic culture.

## Architecture
Redmannish architecture follows a very conventional philosophy. Houses are usually made of wood and have thatched roofs, and are often no more than one floor. In towns, however, houses may be coated with a layer of limewash and have tiled roofs. For these houses, lighting often comes from central hearths.
