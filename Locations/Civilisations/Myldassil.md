The Kingdom of Mÿldassïe is a major elven kingdom in the northernmost lands of the known world. Like other kingdoms in the crown of the world, the landscape thrives in a permanent winter with snow that never seems to melt.

## Architecture
Mÿldassïan buildings are often made of wood, its walls with a stone base. Their roofs are usually built slightly steeper than others, using strips of cured wood with the bark still on.
