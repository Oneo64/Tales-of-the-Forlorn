The Kingdom of Fjellbard is a minor kingdom near the foot of the Elder Mountain Range. The entire kingdom is set inside a murky coniferous landscape, sometimes shrouded in light mist and low clouds.

## Architecture
Compared to its two neighbouring kingdoms, Fjellbard has sacrificed might and power for the comfort of its civilians. Almost every settlement in its land is a town, whether big or small.

Fjellbardic houses are often made using pine logs with thick stone bases, due to the abundance of it near the mountain. They typically have sod roofs and larger windows with wooden shutters, sometimes with an inner locking mechanism for wealthier households.
