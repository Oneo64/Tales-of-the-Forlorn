The Kingdom of Redfall is a major kingdom at the foot of the mountain range named after the kingdom. Even though all of Redfall stretches from the southern area of the mountain to the central area, where two rivers meet, there are no settlements built far from the foothills.

## Architecture
Redmannish architecture follows a very conventional philosophy (comparable to Western Germanic architecture). Houses are usually made of wood and have thatched roofs, and are often no more than one floor. In towns, however, houses may be coated with a layer of limewash and have tiled roofs. For these houses, lighting often comes from central hearths.
