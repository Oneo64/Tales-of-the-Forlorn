# Stormking & Jester Island
The northern islands, Stormking Island and Jester Island, are deserts with nothing but ice mountains and fields of snow. There are no trees here, and lakes are eternally frozen. Glaciers flow from the mountains, and in the north, break apart.

Snowstorms frequently brew in the skies and sweep across the ground, bringing heavy snowfall and strong gales. Sometimes, the storms are so strong that one's vision would be limited to only a hundred metres.

Because of the extreme temperatures, these islands are a prime breeding ground for ice spirits that manifest from the strong storms.
