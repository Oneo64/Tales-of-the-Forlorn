# Helgard Keep
Helgard Keep is a small, stone castle built on a rocky hill, surrounded by a desolate half-tundra. The castle itself is built only for the royalty, where it has everything one needs to live (food still comes from farmers outside), while the residents live in the outer village. Luckily for them, the outer perimeter is secured by a shabby wooden wall with watchtowers and a gate.

Since the outer village is so close to the keep, members of the ruling house occasionally come down to visit and walk around, accompanied by a bodyguard or two.

In the outer village, there is a small rundown tavern owned by an old family; since they don't get many customers, the vicinity is quiet on most days. Unfortunately for travellers, this tavern is the only lodging in the entire kingdom.
