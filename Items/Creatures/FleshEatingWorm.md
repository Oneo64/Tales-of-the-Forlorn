A flesh-eating worm is a monstrous worm of the Unknown World. They live inside forest bushes.

## Description
Flesh-eating worms have pale, greenish-yellow bodies and can grow up to a meter long. Their mouths have small yet sharp copper teeth. If looked at under a light source, you can see their internal organs.

## Abilities
Flesh-eating worms prey on anything that moves (including humans) and can smell blood. Luckily, they have the intelligence of an actual worm.
