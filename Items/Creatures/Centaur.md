A centaur is a half-man, half-horse fey creature. They are often nomadic tribal people, not having a set home.

## Description
Centaurs are a hybrid race of man and horse, with the upper body of a human and the lower body of a horse. They typically have olive skin, black hair, and strong facial features, and their horse hair extends all the way up to their neck and wrists. They mature by 20 and can live up to 150 years old.

## Abilities
Centaurs can gallop, canter, trot, and do horse things just like a normal horse (except neighing).
