A deva is a tribalistic demon of unknown origins. They dwell in small groups in the deserts, preying on unfortunate travellers unlucky enough to stumble onto their grounds at night.

## Description
Devas are towering humanoid demons with blue or red skin, dark yellowed eyes, tusks like a boar, and horns like a goat. They have flat faces. They are usually naked except for a skirt or loincloth.

## Abilities
Devas are stronger and more durable than normal men. They can consume the corpse of a human or elf, rapidly healing themselves in the process. They also have excellent vision in the dark. However, they are nocturnal monsters: weakened by the sunlight, strengthened by the fullness of the moon.

Some devas, who are usually leaders of their group (if they have one), can use sorcery and a special kind of magic to give a sleeping enemy they can see a nightmare.
