A troll is a mysterious forest creature of the north. They dwell in rocky areas inside murky forests, such as a bare hill, a cave entrance, or a cliff face.

## Description
Trolls are slightly shorter than a man, bearing dark hair, black beady eyes that glow in the dark (like a cat), rough grey skin like stone, and large flat noses. They wear mostly browns and greys.

## Abilities
Trolls are much stronger than men, but a lot dumber than humans. Upon stepping into sunlight, they will slowly turn into stone and die if they remain too long, which is why they dwell in dark forests. They can see in the dark.

## Behaviour
Trolls are extremely shy with outsiders and will attack if they feel threatened. For some reason, they tend to move large stones around without people noticing, but they are so stealthy doing it that most people never see them.
