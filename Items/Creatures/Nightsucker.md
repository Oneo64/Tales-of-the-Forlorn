A nightsucker is a haunting spirit that dwells in forests and jungles of the Unknown World.

## Description
Nightsuckers are floating heads with the appearance of a young woman, with her internal organs hanging from the bottom of her neck.

## Abilities
Nightsuckers move by floating in the air and feed on blood. They prefer human blood, but will hunt animals if they cannot find one. During the night, they come out to hunt for prey, only hiding away once day comes.
