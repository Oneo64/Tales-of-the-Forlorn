## Northbardic
Northbardic is spoken in Norgorvalla and Fjellbard. It's basically the same thing as Norgorvallian.

Example of Northbardic: "Hei, min navn er Neo og jag elsker buggan heimer."

## Ranûdan
Ranûdan is spoken in Ranûdel.

Example of Ranûdan: "Ainassareni ôle, massile ere Neo niŋ melya ere gwenden calamôrlai."

## Redmannish
Basically Early Middle English. But for my own sanity's sake, I will just use modern English, with caution for which roots are of Germanic origin and which roots aren't. As for the naming, I will put a native translation somewhere so that the language isn't completely forgotten.

Example of Redmannish: "Hallo, ic nemne Neo and ic lufe maken wuroldes."
Translation: "Hello, my name is neo and I like making worlds."
