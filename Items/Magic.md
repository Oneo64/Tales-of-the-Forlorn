# Magic
Magic is a mysterious force of nature that cannot be explained by even the most revered alchemists in the world. It is widely respected by many. However, getting involved with magic to strengthen oneself can quickly become expensive.

## Note
Using such power drains one's mental energy, causing those who use it excessively to get headaches and seek sleep. Because of this, the magic system will split spells into two categories: weak and strong.

Weak spells mean that casters can use them many times a day on average, while strong spells mean that casters can use them only one or twice a day on average. Of course, mages with more experience can withstand a greater degree of this drainage.

## Clairvoyance
Clairvoyance is the art of foresight and guidance through the use of orbs. To produce an effect, the mage must hold one or two hands over the orb, palms facing it, and chant the required speech.

Clairvoyance orbs are most often made of wood, but richer mages may have one made of a gemstone. Some may have their orb on a staff or wand for ease. However, an ordinary orb is not enough to be worthy of a mage; it has no power. For one to become suitable, it must be specially crafted by soaking it in a special mixture for days.

#### Spells
- **Emotions (weak):** By looking at the target, the caster gains knowledge of their current emotion.
- **Guidance (weak):** The caster gains knowledge of the shortest path towards an item or a place that they have seen, touched, and experienced. If they seek a person, they must know several things about that person. Also, this spell doesn't work if the thing doesn't exist or is extremely far away.
- **Weather (weak):** The caster gains knowledge of the weather's condition for the next week.
- **Thoughts (strong):** By looking at the target, the caster gains knowledge of their current thoughts and intentions.

#### Prevalence
- **Human kingdoms:** Bellaria, Centruma, Grandore
- **Elven kingdoms:** Mÿldassïe, Ranûdel, Zansia

## Galder
Galder is the art of using runic text to grant magical abilities to objects and surfaces. Despite seeming like a simple art, galder must be activated through a short ritual that only mages can perform.

#### Spells
- **Farmward (weak):** The mage must draw a large, intricate symbol on a surface, which must face a farm or a piece of land. Until the symbol fades or is destroyed, no invasive weeds will grow there, and those that are already there will wither and die. Additionally, this sigil also unreliably repels wild animals.
- **Demonward (strong):** The mage must draw a large, intricate symbol on a surface. Whenever a demon or unholy creature enters its vicinity, they are compelled to cower and hide, for the symbol emits a blinding light only they can see.
- **Enchant blade (strong):** The caster must also know wizardry for this spell. By inscribing the incantation of the desired spell onto the blade, it can be activated by another wizard who wields the weapon, and the blade becomes the "orb" of the caster. This spell can also be rigged to be indefinitely active, but requires additional knowledge and effort.

#### Prevalence
- **Human kingdoms:** Fjellbard, Helgard, Norgorvalla
- **Dwarven kingdoms:** Banod, Glomdor, Mornesvid

## Sorcery
Sorcery is the art of turning plain materials into magical effects. In many institutions, sorcery is most commonly taught using powder as the standard. In fact, powder is so common for sorcerers because it brings out the best results. To produce an effect, the mage must hold the material in their hand, make a particular gesture with their other hand, and speak an incantation.

The powder that sorcerers use is mostly a combination of herbs, but not the medicinal kind. Some of the most common combinations include fungi such as lichen, moss, rust, and mushrooms, and weird things such as tree bark, volcanic ash, and even coral.

Spells such as *firebolt* come out of the caster's finger and fly in its direction.

#### Spells
- **Firebolt (weak):** Conjures a small bolt of fire that streaks towards the target.
- **Missile (weak):** Conjures a glowing mote of pure magical force. The caster can change its direction mid-flight, allowing it to hit targets behind walls and adjust its course if the target tries to dodge. At the highest level, this spell can conjure up to five motes in one cast.

#### Prevalence
- **Human kingdoms:** Ardena, Dagad, Shak-Aivan, Taribia
- **Orcish kingdoms:** Neshozia

## Wizardry
Wizardry is the art of using an orb to channel one's power. To produce an effect, the mage must hold the orb in their hand, make a particular gesture with their other hand, and speak an incantation. Without their orb, the mage is powerless.

Wizardry orbs are most often made of wood, but richer mages may have one made of a gemstone. Some may have their orb on a staff or wand for ease. However, an ordinary orb is not enough to be worthy of a mage; it has no power. For one to become suitable, it must be specially crafted by soaking it in a special mixture for days.

Spells such as *firebolt* come out of the actual orb and fly in the direction of the caster's gaze.

#### Spells
- **Firebolt (weak):** Conjures a small bolt of fire that streaks towards the target.
- **Missile (weak):** Conjures a glowing mote of pure magical force. The caster can change its direction mid-flight, allowing it to hit targets behind walls and adjust its course if the target tries to dodge. At the highest level, this spell can conjure up to five motes in one cast.

#### Prevalence
- **Human kingdoms:** Arnland, Bellaria, Centruma, Fjellbard, Grandore, Norgorvalla, Redfall, Vleskyena
- **Elven kingdoms:** Mÿldassïe, Ranûdel, Zansia
